Frequently Asked Questions

What is a URL shortener?

A URL shortener converts a long web address into a shorter link that is easier to copy, share and remember.

Can I use my own custom alias?

Yes. Enable Custom link and enter an available alias using letters, numbers, hyphens or underscores.

Can I add an image and YouTube video?

Yes. Both are optional preview features. The main URL you enter remains the destination visitors are sent to.

Does Shrtigo store my recent links?

The My Links history is stored locally in your browser for convenience. The service also stores the information required to operate a created short link.

Is Shrtigo free to use?

Shrtigo is designed as a free link-shortening service. Features and limits may change as the service develops.
