Contact Shrtigo

We welcome questions, product feedback and reports about links that may violate the service rules.

What to contact us about

• Broken or incorrectly redirecting short links
• Abuse, deceptive or unlawful link reports
• Privacy questions
• Feedback about the Shrtigo service
• Technical problems with creating a short link

How to reach the site owner

Use the contact method associated with this project or the support channel provided by the site owner. When reporting a specific short link, include the short URL and a brief explanation of the issue.

Safety

Never send passwords, payment details, authentication codes or other sensitive information in a support request.
