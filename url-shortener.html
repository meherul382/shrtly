What is a URL shortener?

A URL shortener converts a long web address into a shorter link that redirects visitors to the original destination. Instead of sharing a long address with tracking parameters or complicated paths, you can share a compact URL that is easier to copy, remember and type.

A good URL shortener should make the destination clear to the creator, keep redirects reliable, work well on mobile devices and provide a simple way to create and test links.

Why use a short URL?

Easy to share

Short links are convenient in messages, social posts, email and other places where long URLs are difficult to read.

Easy to remember

A meaningful custom alias can make an important link easier to recognize and communicate.

Cleaner campaigns

Compact URLs can make campaign materials, presentations, flyers and calls to action look less cluttered.

Mobile friendly

Short links are easier to copy, paste and type on phones, especially when the original URL is long.

URL shortener safety tips

A shortened URL can hide the full destination from someone who has not opened it yet. Only shorten and share legitimate destinations, use HTTPS when available, test links before publishing them, and avoid using short URLs to disguise misleading or harmful content.

For visitors, unexpected shortened links should be treated with the same care as any unfamiliar link. Check the context and source before opening important links.

URL shortener FAQs

What is the best free URL shortener?

The right service depends on your needs. For a simple no-signup workflow, compare ease of use, reliability, custom aliases, link management, safety and any usage limits before choosing a service.

Can I shorten any URL?

Shrtigo is intended for normal public website URLs. Destinations should be legitimate and comply with the service's rules.

Can I create a custom short URL?

Yes. Shrtigo supports optional custom aliases, subject to availability and the allowed alias format.

Does a short URL improve Google rankings?

A short URL by itself is not a guarantee of higher rankings. Useful content, crawlability, clear site structure, relevant internal links and a good user experience are more important.

Can I use a short URL on social media?

Yes. Short URLs can be useful in social posts, profiles, messages and campaign links, subject to each platform's policies.
