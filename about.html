About Shrtigo

Shrtigo is a simple URL-shortening service built to make long web addresses easier to share, remember and use across websites, social media, messages, email and other digital channels.

What Shrtigo provides

You can create a short link from a normal HTTP or HTTPS destination, optionally choose a custom alias, and optionally add an image or YouTube preview. The main URL remains the destination for the visitor.

Our approach

We focus on a straightforward interface, clear explanations and responsible link sharing. Short links should make sharing easier without being used to disguise harmful, fraudulent or misleading destinations.

Guides and education

The Shrtigo Guides section provides practical information about URL shorteners, custom aliases, social media sharing, mobile use, marketing, QR codes, safety and troubleshooting.

Service information

Features, limits and technical details may change as Shrtigo develops. For current information, check the site and its published policies.
