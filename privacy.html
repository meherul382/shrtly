Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how Shrtigo handles information when you use the website and create short links.

Information used to operate short links

When you create a short link, Shrtigo stores the destination URL and the optional information needed to provide the requested preview features, such as a YouTube URL and an uploaded image. A short-link code and basic usage information such as click counts may also be stored so the service can operate the link.

Browser storage

The My Links history is stored locally in your browser for convenience. You can remove locally stored history using the controls provided on the site. Local browser storage is separate from the server-side information required to operate a short link.

Uploaded images

If you upload a preview image, the image is stored with the service's storage provider so it can be displayed when the corresponding short link is opened. Do not upload passwords, payment information, private documents or other sensitive information.

Cookies and advertising

Shrtigo may use cookies or similar technologies when required by site functionality or, if advertising is enabled, by advertising and measurement providers. If Google advertising services are enabled on this site, Google and its partners may use cookies to serve or measure ads based on users' visits to this site or other sites, subject to applicable settings and policies.

Users can manage personalized advertising choices through Google's Ads Settings. Users may also review the aboutads.info opt-out options for participating third-party advertising.

Third-party services

Shrtigo may rely on third-party infrastructure for hosting, database, storage, analytics or advertising. These providers may process limited information as necessary to provide their services and are subject to their own privacy policies.

External links

Short links created by users can lead to external websites. Shrtigo does not control the privacy practices or content of third-party destinations, so review the privacy policy of an external site when appropriate.

Data choices

Do not submit sensitive personal information through URL fields or preview uploads. You may delete the local My Links history from your browser. If you need help with a Shrtigo link or privacy concern, use the Contact page.

Policy updates

This policy may be updated when the service, features or legal requirements change.
