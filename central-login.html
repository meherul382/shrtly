Everything you can do with Shrtigo

One premium dashboard for creating, managing and monitoring your short links. Shrtigo is built to keep your link workflow simple, organized and easy to control.

🔗 Shorten Any URL

Create clean, shareable short links from long website URLs. Your destination remains under your control while the short URL is easier to share.

🌐 Multiple Short-Link Domains

Choose an available Shrtigo domain when creating a link. This lets you organize different links across your supported branded domains.

✏️ Custom Alias

Create a memorable short address with an optional custom alias instead of relying only on a randomly generated code.

🖼️ Image & YouTube Preview

Add optional image or YouTube preview media to supported links when you want a richer sharing experience before the destination opens.

📊 Click Analytics

View click activity and top-performing short links from your private dashboard, including today, yesterday and all-time link performance.

🔗 My Links Management

Keep track of created links and use Copy, Edit and Delete controls to manage your saved links from one place.

💳 Flexible Plans

Start with the available free allowance and upgrade when you need more capacity. Paid plans provide larger click limits or unlimited-click periods according to the selected plan.

🎁 500-Click Welcome Gift

New accounts can claim the Welcome Gift for 500 free clicks. When the free allowance is used, you can choose an upgrade plan to continue.

🌐 Domain Management

Manage your supported branded domains from the Domains section and select an available domain while creating short links.

🔔 Notifications & Support

Stay informed about Shrtigo features, account updates and service information from the Notifications area, with support available through the account panel.

How Shrtigo works

1. Create an accountSign up for free and access your private Shrtigo dashboard.
2. Create your short linkEnter a destination URL, choose your options and create the short link.
3. Share & monitorShare the short URL and use Analytics and My Links to manage your activity.
Built for simple link management. Shrtigo brings short links, domains, analytics, plans and account tools together in one place, so you can manage your links without switching between multiple tools.
