URL Shortener for Bloggers: Sharing Tips

Bloggers often share article URLs across social media, newsletters and communities. A short link can make those addresses easier to copy and remember.

Use short links for campaigns

Create a dedicated short URL for an article, resource or campaign and keep the original page as the destination.

Keep aliases descriptive

Use a readable alias when it helps people understand the link. Avoid misleading wording.

Do not expect automatic SEO gains

Shortening a URL does not by itself improve search rankings. Search visibility depends on useful content, crawlability, site quality and other signals.

Test every important link

Check the destination on desktop and mobile before including it in a newsletter or printed material.
