How to Make a Short Link for a Website

A website URL can be shortened into a compact address that is easier to copy and share. Shrtigo accepts normal public website URLs and creates a short redirect.

Step 1: Copy your website URL

Copy the complete HTTPS address of the page you want visitors to open.

Step 2: Paste it into Shrtigo

Paste the address into the main URL field and select Shorten Link.

Step 3: Customize if useful

Enable Custom link when a memorable alias would help. Keep it short and accurately related to the destination.

Step 4: Test and share

Open the generated address before publishing. Then share it in messages, email, websites or social media with helpful context.
