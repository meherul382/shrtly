How to Choose a Good URL Shortener: 12 Things to Check

Not every URL shortener offers the same features or policies. Use this checklist before choosing one for everyday sharing or important campaigns.

1. Ease of use

The basic workflow should be clear and quick.

2. Custom aliases

If memorable links matter, check whether custom codes are supported.

3. Redirect reliability

Important links depend on the service staying available.

4. HTTPS

Use secure web connections and HTTPS destinations whenever possible.

5. Privacy

Read the service's privacy policy and understand what information is stored.

6. Link management

Check whether the service offers the history or management features you actually need.

7. Media options

If previews matter, check whether images or videos are supported and how they behave.

8. Mobile experience

The service should work well on phones as well as desktop browsers.

9. Domain reputation

Consider whether the short domain is recognizable and trustworthy to your audience.

10. Terms of service

Know which destinations and uses are prohibited.

11. Transparency

Clear documentation and policies are useful indicators of responsible service.

12. Long-term availability

For printed campaigns, consider how dependent you are on the short-link provider remaining available.
