URL Shortener for Students: Simple Sharing Guide

Students often share documents, forms, project pages and learning resources through messaging apps. Short URLs can make those addresses easier to copy and remember.

Useful student examples

You can shorten a public project page, a class resource, an online form or another legitimate educational destination.

Check access first

Make sure the underlying document or page has the correct sharing permissions. A short link does not change access rules.

Test before sharing

Open the generated link and confirm that classmates can reach the intended resource without unexpected redirects.

Stay safe

Do not share passwords or confidential information through public URLs. Be cautious with unexpected shortened links received from others.
