How to Create a Short Link for Instagram Bio

Instagram profiles are often used to send people to a website, store, portfolio or campaign. A short URL can be easier to remember and share in a bio.

Choose the right destination

Decide exactly which public page visitors should open. Avoid changing the destination without checking the link after a campaign starts.

Create a memorable URL

Paste your destination into Shrtigo and optionally select a custom alias. Keep the alias short, descriptive and honest.

Test on mobile

Open the generated URL on a phone and confirm that the destination loads correctly. Mobile testing matters because Instagram traffic is commonly mobile.

Improve the bio experience

Explain what the link leads to and use consistent wording across your profile and other marketing channels.
