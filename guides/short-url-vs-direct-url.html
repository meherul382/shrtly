Short URL vs Direct URL: Which Should You Use?

A direct URL shows the destination address, while a short URL uses a compact code that redirects to the destination. Both can be useful.

FactorShort URLDirect URL
SharingCompactCan be long
TransparencyDestination may be hiddenDestination is visible
MaintenanceDepends on the shortenerDepends on the destination

When a short URL helps

Short links are useful in messages, printed material, social posts and places where a long address is difficult to read.

When a direct URL is better

Use the direct address when maximum destination transparency matters or when you want to avoid a dependency on a shortening service.

Best approach

Choose based on context. Whichever format you use, test the destination and share it honestly.
