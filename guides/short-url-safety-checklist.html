Short URL Safety Checklist Before You Share

Short URLs are convenient, but the compact address may hide the final destination. Use this checklist before publishing an important link.

Safety checklist

  • Confirm the final destination is correct.
  • Prefer an HTTPS destination.
  • Open the short link yourself before sharing it.
  • Use an honest, descriptive alias.
  • Never place passwords or sensitive data in a public URL.
  • Review sharing permissions for private resources.
  • Be cautious with unexpected shortened links from strangers.

For campaign links

Keep a record of the original destination and test important links after changes. A short URL should make sharing easier, not make the destination deceptive.
