Best Free URL Shorteners: What to Look For

The best free URL shortener depends on your use case. Before choosing one, compare reliability, link controls, privacy, ease of use and the features you actually need.

1. Reliable redirects

A short link is useful only when it consistently reaches the intended destination.

2. Custom aliases

Readable aliases can make campaign and resource links easier to remember, provided the service supports them.

3. Clear policies

Read the service's terms and privacy information so you understand how created links and analytics are handled.

4. Safety features

Creators and recipients should be able to recognize suspicious or misleading destinations. Never use shortening to disguise harmful content.

5. Easy sharing

A clean interface, copy button and mobile-friendly workflow can make a practical difference.
