How to Create a Short Link for LinkedIn

A short URL can make a LinkedIn profile, company page or article address easier to share in messages and marketing materials.

Copy the public LinkedIn URL

Choose the public page you want to share and copy its address.

Create the short link

Paste it into Shrtigo and generate a short URL. A clear custom alias can make the destination easier to recognize.

Test before posting

Open the generated URL and verify that it reaches the intended LinkedIn page. Add context to the post so readers know where the link goes.
