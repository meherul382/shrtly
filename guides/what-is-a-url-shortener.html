What Is a URL Shortener? A Complete Beginner's Guide

A URL shortener turns a long web address into a shorter link that is easier to copy, share and remember. Instead of sending a complicated address with many characters, you can share a compact link that redirects visitors to the original destination.

How does a URL shortener work?

A shortening service creates a unique short code and associates it with the destination URL. When someone opens the short link, the service looks up that code and sends the visitor to the original address. The visible short link and the destination are therefore two different addresses connected by a redirect.

Why use a short URL?

Short links are convenient in social posts, messages, email, printed material and marketing campaigns. They can be easier to read aloud and less likely to wrap awkwardly in places where space is limited. A custom alias can also make a link more memorable.

Are shortened URLs safe?

A short URL can hide the final destination, so users should only click links from sources they trust. Website owners should use HTTPS destinations, test their links before sharing, and avoid misleading aliases. A shortened link should never be used to disguise harmful or deceptive content.
